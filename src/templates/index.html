SWGMAT / ASTM E2290

Record observations about the signature or handwriting — pen lifts, tracing marks, proportional anomalies, ink flow irregularities, comparison with known exemplars.

📋 Paper & Substrate Anomalies
ISO/IEC 14443 / ASTM E2289

Document physical paper anomalies — missing or misaligned watermarks, security thread issues, paper texture inconsistencies, UV fluorescence anomalies, indentation marks.

Physical examination findings including tactile observations are relevant here.

🖊️ Ink / Toner Alteration
ASTM E2926 / SWGMAT Ink Standards

Note any ink-related anomalies — feathering, chemical erasure, obliteration, overwriting, correction fluid residue, toner adhesion issues, multiple ink types.

Include observations from ESDA, IR reflectography, or chemical tests if performed.

For digital documents — timestamp discrepancies, author field mismatches, software version anomalies, hash mismatches, absent digital signatures.

Leave blank for physical-only documents without a digital component.
